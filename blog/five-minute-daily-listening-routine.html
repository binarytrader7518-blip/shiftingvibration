Why five minutes works

Small habits stick because they're easy to start. The seller's directions ask for at least five minutes of listening a day. That's short enough to fit around work, family and errands, and long enough to feel like a genuine pause.

Step 1: Attach it to an existing habit

Pick something you already do every day, like your morning coffee, your commute as a passenger or brushing your teeth at night. Press play straight after it. Anchoring the new habit to an old one removes the need to remember.

Step 2: Choose your default track

Decision fatigue kills routines. The included guide suggests starting with the Golden Ratio track, so make that your default for the first week. Once the habit is in place, explore Shaman Beats for meditation or Stormy Escape for evenings.

Step 3: Make it comfortable

Use headphones for binaural tracks, keep the volume moderate and silence notifications. Sitting or lying down is ideal, but the seller also suggests listening while walking or cooking.

Step 4: Track 14 days

The seller recommends listening 14 days in a row. Keep a one-line log each day: track, time and how you felt. After two weeks you'll know which tracks you reach for and whether the habit is worth keeping. You also have the seller's 60-day guarantee if it isn't.
