Why sound helps you pause

Relaxation techniques aim to trigger the body's relaxation response, the opposite of the stress reaction. NCCIH lists slow breathing, guided imagery and other methods that work toward this. Pairing them with music gives your attention something steady to rest on, which makes the practice easier to begin.

A five-minute calm-down

Sit comfortably, play Golden Ratio Experience at a moderate volume and breathe in for four counts and out for six. Let your shoulders drop on each exhale. When the five minutes are up, notice one thing that feels different. Over two weeks, the track itself becomes a cue that it's time to slow down.

What customers describe

Reviewers on the seller's page mention feeling more grounded, safer and less stressed after listening. These are personal experiences, not clinical outcomes, but they show how many people use the collection.
